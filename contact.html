Support is provided by email. No street address or telephone number is published on these pages; if a regional Google Play Trader profile requires business contact details separately, those are submitted directly in Google Play Console.

App support and bug reports

Because SuperPotli has zero internet permissions and includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools, the app never sends logs or diagnostics automatically. When reporting an issue to Sproject01@proton.me, please include:

  • The name of the tool you were using.
  • Your Android version and phone model.
  • A brief description of what happened and what you expected.

Please avoid attaching sensitive personal files unless necessary to explain an issue.

Lifetime Pro billing and restore help

For help with Lifetime Pro (superpotli_pro), purchase restoration, or refund questions after 48 hours, email Sproject01@proton.me and include:

  • Your Google Play order ID (GPA.xxxx-...) from your Google Play receipt or pay.google.com.
  • Confirmation that you are signed into the Google Play account that made the purchase.

Reminder: Lifetime Pro is a single one-time purchase with all future Pro updates included, no ads, no subscriptions, no free trial, and no automatic recurring payments. You can also tap Restore purchase inside Settings > Pro anytime while briefly online.

Privacy and open-source notices

  • Privacy inquiries: S Projects collects no personal data inside SuperPotli. If you email Sproject01@proton.me, we use your email address and message only to reply and delete them once your request is resolved.
  • Open-source source offer: SuperPotli includes the LAME 3.100 MP3 encoder (LGPL) in libsuperpotli_mp3.so. You can download the source archive via our Open-Source Notices page or request a copy by emailing Sproject01@proton.me.
