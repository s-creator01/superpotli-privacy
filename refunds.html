About Lifetime Pro

SuperPotli includes 185 offline tools across 12 categories: 145 Free tools and 40 Pro tools. Three Free tool families also have limits lifted by Pro:

  • Video tools: up to 5 minutes per clip in Free (unlimited length in Pro).
  • Audio editing tools: up to 15 minutes per clip in Free (unlimited length in Pro).
  • Resize Image and Compress Image: up to 10 pictures at once in Free (up to 300 pictures at once in Pro).

Lifetime Pro (superpotli_pro) is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed through Google Play Billing. It unlocks all 40 Pro tools, removes the three Free limits, and includes all future Pro updates. There are no ads now or in the future, no subscriptions, no free trial, no automatic recurring payments, and no mandatory login or account.

Requesting a refund within 48 hours (Google Play)

All SuperPotli Pro payments are processed directly by Google Play. Under Google Play's refund policy (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637), you may request a refund through Google Play within 48 hours of purchase:

  • Open play.google.com or the Google Play Store app, sign in with the Google account used for the purchase, go to Payments & subscriptions > Budget & order history, find SuperPotli Pro, and select Request a refund.
  • Refund eligibility and final approval are determined solely by Google Play under Google Play's refund rules; refunds are not guaranteed.
  • For buyers in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, purchasing digital content that unlocks immediately may waive the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal as disclosed during Google Play checkout.

Billing issues or requests after 48 hours

If more than 48 hours have passed since your purchase, or if you experience a billing issue (such as a pending charge or Pro not unlocking after payment), please email Sproject01@proton.me with your Google Play order ID (which starts with GPA.xxxx-...) from your Google Play receipt email or pay.google.com.

Because S Projects runs no payment servers and Google Play handles all transactions, we cannot guarantee refunds outside Google Play's refund policy, but we will review your order ID and help resolve entitlement or billing problems.

Restoring your purchase before requesting a refund

If you reinstalled SuperPotli, cleared app data, or switched to a new Android phone, you do not need to buy Pro again:

  • Make sure your device is signed into the exact Google Play account that originally purchased Lifetime Pro.
  • Connect briefly to the internet so Google Play can verify your purchase locally through Binder IPC, then open Settings > Pro (or the Lifetime Pro screen) and tap Restore purchase.
  • Once verified, SuperPotli caches only minimal local state (pro_owned, pro_verified_at, and pro_refund_notice) in private DataStore so Pro keeps working completely offline. If Google Play reports a purchase as refunded, Pro unlocks turn off and all 145 Free tools keep working.
