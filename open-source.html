Complete third-party licence texts are also available offline inside the app under Settings > About > Licenses.

MP3 Encoder Source Offer (LAME 3.100 — LGPL v2+)

When you convert or extract audio to MP3 offline, SuperPotli loads a separate native library (libsuperpotli_mp3.so) built from the LAME 3.100 MP3 encoder (lame.sourceforge.io, licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2 or later) and a small JNI bridge written for SuperPotli.

In accordance with the LGPL, the complete corresponding source archive for libsuperpotli_mp3.so (containing the LAME 3.100 source tarball, SHA-256 checksum, JNI bridge source, Android NDK build script, LGPL licence text, and rebuild instructions) is available for direct download below. Everything else in SuperPotli remains proprietary ("All rights reserved"). This source offer remains valid for at least three years following distribution of any version containing this library. If you need assistance obtaining the archive, email Sproject01@proton.me.
